Snap64RecompVR
Experimental Windows PC VR build

1. CONNECT YOUR HEADSET
Use your PC's active OpenXR runtime with the headset connected. Quest uses
a PC connection; this is not a standalone Quest application.

2. PROVIDE YOUR ROM
Place your own Pokemon Snap (USA) cartridge dump in this folder as
pokemonsnap.z64, or select your dump when the program asks. No ROM is included.

3. START IN VR
Double-click Snap64RecompVR.exe. VR starts by default, with no launch arguments.
Keep assets/vr and the included DLLs beside the executable.
For desktop play, run: Snap64RecompVR.exe --desktop

4. CONTROLS
Point and pull trigger or press A/X to confirm menus; B/Y goes back.
Hold grip near the right holster to pick up the camera. Pull that hand's
trigger to take a photo; move its thumbstick up/down to zoom. Release grip
to return the camera. Grip an unlocked item at its dispenser and release
to throw. Both dispensers are on your right.
Press your empty hand into the round flute button on the left dashboard;
no trigger is needed. It stays gray and inactive until the flute is unlocked.
Press A/X to continue dialogue, hold B/Y to dash, and use the left menu
button to pause where the runtime makes it available.
Click the left thumbstick for VR settings. Press both thumbsticks together
while looking forward to recenter.
The original game's Options entries and pages are hidden in VR; they are
available in --desktop mode.

README.md contains the full VR controls table. docs/VR.md covers setup,
settings and the limits of current headset validation.

Keep saves/pokemonsnap.bin and saves/vr-photo-lenses.json together when
moving your saves. LICENSE, NOTICE.md and licenses/ contain the code and
dependency licensing information; assets/vr/NOTICE.md credits the VR assets.
